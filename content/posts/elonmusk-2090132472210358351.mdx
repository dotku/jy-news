Grok Voice Think Fast 2.0 just beat GPT Realtime across both text and voice on VulcanBench

And the voice result is what makes this especially impressive



Grok preserves almost all of that performance when moving from typed questions to spoken ones, while still completing the full response dramatically faster



So Grok is not just getting better at voice



It’s becoming a seriously powerful realtime reasoning model that can listen, think and respond incredibly fast
