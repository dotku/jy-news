BREAKING: California Birth Rate Falls Below Replacement Level



• California’s fertility rate hit a record low

• It fell from 2.21 children per woman in 2007 to 1.48 in 2023

• That’s far below the 2.1 replacement level

• By 2038, deaths could outnumber births in California

• Public school enrollment could drop by about 630,000 students
