Our labs keep trying to spin this into a push for broader regulation. 



It can and should backfire. 



The models are not "going rogue" or acting of their own accord, like they're some Marvel movie evil robot. 



People made bad harnesses, told them to hack things and had transparently and objectively bad dev-ops and security practices. 



The fact that folks are trying to spin this into a "we need help from the government to regulate everyone" instead of "we should be punished in a narrow way on these specific incidents under existing law" is the real disconnect right now.
