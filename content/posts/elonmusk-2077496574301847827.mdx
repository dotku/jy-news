Starlink and the https://nitter.net/JAAGOFoundation in Bangladesh are providing connectivity to 30,000 students across remote schools, giving each child the opportunity to develop digital skills that were not possible before 🛰️💓


JAAGO Foundation (@JAAGOFoundation)

In collaboration with https://nitter.net/Starlink, we are redefining access to education—bringing seamless, uninterrupted learning to 167 classrooms across 26 districts. By bridging the digital divide, we are breaking down geographical barriers and opening the door to quality education for 30,000 students, strengthening our commitment to ensure every child has equal rights and opportunities.
