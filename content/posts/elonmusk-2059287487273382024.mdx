Paste screenshots into Grok Build


X Freeze (@XFreeze)

Just press Cmd + Shift + Ctrl + 3, then Cmd + V paste the screenshot directly into Grok Build and ask it to inspire, guide, explain, debug, or recreate what you want



It’s much easier to explain ideas visually instead of trying to type out every tiny detail manually



The workflow feels incredibly natural and easy once you start using images directly inside the development process



Grok is multimodal and already has surprisingly strong vision capabilities for understanding screenshots, UI, layouts, and visual context
