Music is missing its "openai-whisper". It's time that we can turn any music into notes, the same way transcribing speech is now a given. Are we there yet? Let us know after testing MuScriptor 🎶 Work by https://nitter.net/simonrouard (https://nitter.net/kyutai_labs, https://nitter.net/Ircam) and Michael Krause (https://nitter.net/MireloAI).


kyutai (@kyutai_labs)

We're releasing MuScriptor, the best open model for multi-instrument transcription to date, created in collaboration with https://nitter.net/MireloAI.

Give it a recording in any genre: pop, classical, metal, jazz, whatever, and it transcribes the individual instruments into MIDI. Link in 🧵
