Half price Starlink for people in the Memphis region


Sawyer Merritt (@SawyerMerritt)

SpaceXAI has announced that they will be applying a 50% discount on the standard https://nitter.net/Starlink monthly price for both new and existing customers in the Memphis area as a way to give back to the community where they operate their Colossus data centers.



• No upfront hardware costs on Residential Starlink kits for new customers

• The discount applies to Starlink service addresses located in eligible parts of the Memphis area (see below). 

• No action is required. The discount will appear on your next bill and will continue as long as your service address remains in the eligible area. 

• Friends and family nearby can also benefit from the discount. You both earn a month of free service through Starlink's referral program.
