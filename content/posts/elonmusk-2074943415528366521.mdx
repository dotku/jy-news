Wow… within 5 minutes my kids built this game called the Tesla City with Grok 4.5. 



It’s a simple Tesla arcade game in SF where:



1/ You can choose a Tesla (3 / Y / S / X / Cybertruck / Roadster / Semi / Cybercab)



2/ Give robotaxi rides - pickup and drop people off for $



3/ Can upgrade your car with the $ (buy new weapons, etc.) 



4/ Fight gas cars with blasters, homing missiles, EMP & plasma, as they try to attack you to prevent you from completing Robotaxi rides.
