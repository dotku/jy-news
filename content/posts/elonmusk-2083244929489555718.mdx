Tesla now has a dedicated live Virtual Power Plant dashboard for multiple regions: California, Texas, New England, Australia and Puerto Rico.



In total, Tesla has 226,158 homes participating with a total capacity of 2.3 GW.



You can even go back in time to a specific grid event/date and see how many Powerwall units dispatched, energy output for that event, etc. Pretty cool.
