Lots to do together. Excited to be joining forces with https://nitter.net/SpaceX to build useful AI.


SpaceX (@SpaceX)

SpaceX has exercised the option to acquire https://nitter.net/cursor_ai in an all-stock transaction with the goal of building the world’s most useful AI models.



For the past few months, SpaceXAI has been jointly training a model with Cursor, which will be released in Cursor and Grok Build soon.



We look forward to working closely with the Cursor team to advance our frontier AI capabilities
