BREAKING: Emirates is reportedly removing Viasat equipment from its new Airbus A350s as it moves to standardize its fleet on Starlink.



• Incoming A350s are expected to transition from Viasat GX Aviation to Starlink

• Starlink is already active on dozens of Emirates Boeing 777s and a growing number of A380s

• Emirates plans to equip approximately 232 aircraft by mid-2027

• Passengers have already made more than 1 million Starlink connections

• More than 1 petabyte of data has been used since the rollout began.
