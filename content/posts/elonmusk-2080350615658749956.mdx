Elon Musk explains the real constraint on scaling Tesla Robotaxi:



“Our goals for Robotaxi are very ambitious, but we need to be extremely cautious about causing an accident or harming anyone



There are roughly 30,000 to 40,000 automotive deaths every year in the United States, and most of them receive almost no press coverage



But if Tesla injures even one person, it becomes worldwide headline news, and regulators could immediately clamp down on our operations



We do not want to injure anyone



We are scaling Robotaxi as fast as humanly possible while trying to ensure that we cause no harm at all and ideally do not even run over a pet



That is the constraint: grow Robotaxi as quickly as possible without harming anyone”
