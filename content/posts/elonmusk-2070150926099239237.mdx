Tesla has announced that it will increase production output at its Giga Berlin factory by another 20% to 7,500 vehicles per week starting in October of this year.



This is in addition to the 20% production output increase previously announced a few months ago.



Tesla will hire 1,000 additional employees by October for this new increase in production.



"3,500 additional jobs will be created in the short and medium term for vehicle and battery manufacturing at the plant," Tesla said.
