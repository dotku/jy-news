The answer is always 0

Some people really do care about the causes (doubtful that ro or warren or aoc or Bernie do at all) but regardless the core moral error is they think that being the one to demand where money is spent is a morally good action. 



It isn’t.



Service is service. Sacrifice is sacrifice. Generosity is generosity. Secondhand is not. Controlling is not.
