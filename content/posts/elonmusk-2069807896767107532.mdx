BREAKING: Starlink will help power emergency communications across all 78 Puerto Rico municipalities.



• Announced by Puerto Rico Governor Jenniffer González Colón



• Puerto Rico will equip emergency management offices with Starlink satellite internet.



• Starlink will donate satellite antennas to Puerto Rico’s emergency management bureau



• The deployment will cover all 78 municipalities



• Designed to keep communications online during hurricanes, disasters, and emergencies



• Provides backup internet when cellular and fiber networks fail



• Helps emergency responders coordinate rescue and recovery efforts during outages



This is why Starlink matters: internet when everything else goes down.
