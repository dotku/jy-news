Elon Musk on why tunnels became reality:



“Everyone thought it was a joke. We made it a company”



The idea behind The Boring Company is actually simple physics and geometry:



Cities are becoming vertically 3D with massive skyscrapers and dense urban centers, yet transportation systems are still mostly trapped in 2D surface streets



That mismatch creates unavoidable congestion



A fully 3D transport network with tunnels running beneath cities could theoretically scale to almost any traffic level while dramatically reducing surface congestion



Elon says there’s still enormous opportunity in tunneling and surprisingly only few companies taking it seriously
