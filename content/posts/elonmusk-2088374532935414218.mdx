I have a clean Under the Hood report for the month of July! 🥳



I used to always wonder about my reach and visibility and was convinced something was wrong with my account. But it turns out my assumptions were wrong.



According to my Under the Hood report, X found zero labels on my account or any of my posts that would have limited my reach during July.



That means nothing in the system flagged my account or content in a way that reduced its distribution in the For You feed.



My posts were not subject to any of the visibility restrictions tracked by the report.



So for the entire month of July 2026, I had a completely clean Under the Hood report: zero reach-limiting labels at either the account or post level. 🥳



Sometimes low reach really is just… low reach. 😅



I just love this new feature. It gives us a little more transparency into what’s actually happening with our accounts instead of leaving us guessing.
