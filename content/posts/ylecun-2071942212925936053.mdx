We can finally say AI isn't killing jobs.



A new paper from me, https://nitter.net/tryramp, and https://nitter.net/RevelioLabs uses firm-level spend and workforce data across 21K U.S. businesses to measure AI's impact on jobs.



Firms that adopt AI heavily grow headcount 10% over two years following adoption. Low adopters see no statistically significant change.
