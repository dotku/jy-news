The word "steampunk" was coined in April 1987 by American science fiction writer K. W. Jeter. 



He proposed it jokingly in a letter to *Locus* magazine to describe the alternate-history novels set in the 19th century that he was writing alongside his fellow authors Tim Powers and James Blaylock.
