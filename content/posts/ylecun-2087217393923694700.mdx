A story in light of the recent math AI results.

Last December I invited Julia Kempe (https://nitter.net/KempeLab ) to the podcast and asked her what she wanted to talk about. She said: now that math has fallen, what should researchers run after?



This was last December. Models were nowhere near solving open problems. So I asked, do you mean we should talk about what AI can currently solve in math?

She said, no, Ravid. Math as we know it has changed forever, and so has the work of mathematicians. The problem is they don't know it yet.



Today that sounds obvious. Back then it was just one more example of a brilliant scientist seeing where things were going.
