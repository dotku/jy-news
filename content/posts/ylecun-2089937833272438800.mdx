inspired by the anthropic post, i made a protein font!



how does it work? when you design by differentiating backward through a folding model like esm-fold2 you can ask that the distogram of the folded protein looks like something you want. 



a little bit of help from https://nitter.net/ChatGPT and now we have a protein font (fable, of course, didn't want to help me).



this was not fully automated, i had to dial in some details like the protein sizes etc, but jointly me and codex got it working.
