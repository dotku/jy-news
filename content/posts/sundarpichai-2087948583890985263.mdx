Our Flash models are workhorses that offer performance at a great price. So, we're shipping updates fast to get them in developers’ hands. And now just 3 weeks after launching 3.6 Flash, 3.7 Flash shows significant gains including in coding and agentic work. 



It delivers substantial improvements across software engineering, knowledge work, and web development, all with an introductory price at half the original cost of 3.6 Flash.



We've gotten positive feedback from early users, including our own engineers using 3.7 in https://nitter.net/Antigravity, and we’re excited for you to try it there. Also available via Gemini API in https://nitter.net/GoogleAIStudio, Gemini Enterprise & more, full list and details here: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-3-7-flash/
