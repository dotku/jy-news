Starlink enables fast, reliable connectivity where traditional infrastructure does not exist 🛰️🏔️


OffRoadCampingBoys (@OffRoadCampBoys)

Many of the areas we went, there is not a single cell provider that has reception for long distances.  Here is a speed test location at one camp site. And, yes, the more remote the better it performs.  We ran Starlink for 8,431 miles - non-stop and turned off cellular data to prevent switching and dropping from the network.
