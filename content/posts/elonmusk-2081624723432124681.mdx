I don’t pay the protection money to the media


John LeFevre (@JohnLeFevre)

I sat on the syndicate desk of a global investment bank from the dot-com crash through the global financial crisis.



We had to do a lot of media work to promote deals. One day I asked our Global Head of Communications why the media was so gentle with us.



He laughed:

“Because we’re one of their biggest advertisers.”



That explains a lot of the media’s treatment of Elon.



Tesla is so successful, its ad spend is effectively zero, or ~0.01% of the $40 billion global auto advertising market.



No ad dollars = no protection.



No one knows this better than Big Pharma.



They don't make confusing and enigmatic ads to sell you product; they make the ads to control the media.
