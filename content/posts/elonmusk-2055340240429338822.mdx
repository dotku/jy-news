STARLINK: Today, SpaceX Sr. Director of Starlink Market Access & Development, Ryan Goodnight, and Executive Director of Uganda Communications Commission, Nyombi Thembo, signed a Memorandum of Understanding and operational license agreement. 



The agreement allows SpaceX to begin Starlink operations as they agree to comply with Uganda’s laws and regulatory requirements.
