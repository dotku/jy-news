As models get even better, the need for transparency grows. Last year https://nitter.net/nvidia adopted our SynthID invisible watermark, and today we’re excited to announce https://nitter.net/OpenAI, Kakao, and https://nitter.net/ElevenLabs will join them.



We’re also going further by adding C2PA Content Credentials verification to https://nitter.net/GeminiApp alongside Synth ID detection and bringing both to Search and Chrome so you can easily check whether content was captured by a camera, or created/ edited with gen AI tools.
