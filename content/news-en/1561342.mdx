《Forza Horizon 6》Radio Stations

《Forza Horizon 6》will feature the largest number of songs in the series to date, including 9 radio stations covering electronic dance music, energetic drum and bass, relaxed electronic pop, powerful rock, alternative indie music, hip-hop rhythms, nostalgic synthwave, and modern classical music. Here is a list of all the radio stations:

Horizon Pulse

Horizon Bass Arena

Horizon Block Party

Horizon XS

Hospital Records

Gacha City Radio

Sub Pop Records

Horizon Wave

Horizon Opus

《Forza Horizon 6》Accessibility Features

Regarding the accessibility features of 《Forza Horizon 6》, the game will include features such as automatic driving, close proximity radar (to help identify vehicles in blind spots), and high contrast mode (to help players more easily distinguish objects).

Players will also experience some returning features, such as full controller key remapping, screen reading, subtitles, adjustable field of view options on PC, colorblind filters, offline game speed adjustments, and our full range of difficulty settings and driving assists.

After the game launches, players will see American Sign Language (ASL) and British Sign Language (BSL) added to cutscenes, displayed as picture-in-picture ASL or BSL commentary.
