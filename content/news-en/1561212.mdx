According to blogger @project_vice on May 3, 2024, Rockstar is reportedly entering the early stages of physical marketing production, with posters, billboards, and game retail packaging all in preparation.

The key clue supporting this claim is a LinkedIn job posting from Rockstar Games' New York branch. The position is for a temporary Production Artist. Industry insiders point out that these short-term positions are usually not for maintaining the daily operation of the studio, but are specifically established for a particular marketing campaign or product production batch.

This recruitment move coincides with the rumors of physical marketing materials being produced, suggesting that the global marketing and promotion of "GTA6" is no longer a distant concept, but is currently underway with personnel building and substantial progress.

Just days before the above news broke, Take-Two CEO Zelnich made a statement regarding "GTA6" pre-orders, stating that the company is carefully considering the final pricing strategy for consumers.

In the general release pace of the gaming industry, the production of physical marketing materials and the announcement of prices and pre-order information are usually carried out in parallel, not in isolation. The two are often closely linked. Therefore, the appearance of signals from both aspects within the same time window has attracted particular attention.

Further fueling market expectations is the expected Take-Two earnings call, which will be held in approximately two and a half weeks. Historically, earnings calls have been the occasion for companies to update product timelines, pricing strategies, and commercial progress in a more concrete form than executive interviews.

It should be noted that, as of now, in addition to the information already confirmed by Zelnick in interviews, the above description of the production stage is still mainly based on a comprehensive analysis of social media rumors and job posting clues, and has not been officially confirmed by Rockstar or Take-Two.
