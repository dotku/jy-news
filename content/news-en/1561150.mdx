Today, Zhang Xue posted a message stating that everyone wants to win, and we all have shortcomings and areas for improvement. He welcomes rational criticism and assures everyone they will strive to grow and improve, but also requests that fans maintain basic courtesy towards all brands.

In a video, Zhang Xue urged everyone to remain calm: "Don't, because we won, go and put down other brands, that is definitely wrong."

Zhang Xue admitted that being able to participate in the race and win the championship demonstrates that Zhang Xue motorcycle's product performance and R&D have reached a high level, at least reaching a level where it can compete with others.

However, to be honest, large-scale manufacturing quality management systems and R&D are two separate systems, and Zhang Xue is truly very, very lacking in this area. Compared to international first-tier brands, our management may still be far behind.

Zhang Xue stated that he will definitely work hard to learn and grow rapidly, but one principle he will absolutely adhere to is to admit mistakes, stand at attention when criticized, and then quickly improve, so as not to disappoint the trust and support of every user of Zhang Xue.

It is worth mentioning that the Hungary race was Zhang Xue motorcycle's fourth race, and Zhang Xue motorcycle has officially bid farewell to the preferential treatment period for new cars in the first three races. This victory further proves that Zhang Xue motorcycle still has top-level competitiveness without any preferential treatment.

The second race of the WSBK Hungary WorldSSP race will officially start at 8 PM Beijing time tonight. Next, Zhang Xue motorcycle will also participate in the World Superbike Championship Czech round on the 15th.
