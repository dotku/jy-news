Before the update

The new function not only supports fast transfer of images and files between multiple devices but also supports face-to-face instant transfer, making the usage more flexible.

After the update

After entering the "Air Transfer" interface, users can either select associated devices to send files or directly transfer files to other users, achieving a more convenient cross-end and near-field sharing experience.

Looking back, the WeChat Input Method was first launched on Android and iOS platforms in December 2022 as "WeChat Keyboard 1.0.0," and was officially renamed WeChat Input Method in June 2023, with continuous version iterations.

In December 2025, the iOS version of WeChat Input Method received a major 3.0 version update, focusing on upgrading voice input capabilities, introducing more advanced large model technology, and achieving significant improvements in recognition accuracy and input speed.

It is worth mentioning that Zhang Xiaolong, Tencent Senior Vice President and President of the WeChat Business Group, once stated that the team's purpose in launching the WeChat Input Method is not to compete with other input method applications for market share, but to better protect user privacy.

Overall, with the addition of functions such as "Air Transfer," the WeChat Input Method is gradually extending from a single input tool to a cross-device collaboration and lightweight file transfer tool.
