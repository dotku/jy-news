Jia Yueting stated that during the Buffett shareholder meeting, he will jointly establish an AI and Robotics Research Institute with BIBS Boston International Business School, and connect with the University of California, Los Angeles, to lay out all-American campus cooperation, robot procurement, and EAI education summer camps, building a scalable AI education ecosystem.

In addition, FF's EAI bridge strategy has been recognized by the Wall Street Journal, which praised it as an important development path for connecting the global EAI industry, AI technology, and user value.

Public information shows that EAI Robot is the embodied intelligence robot product line launched by Faraday Future (FF).

Faraday Future announced its entry into this field on January 8, 2026, and released the Futurist, Master, and Aegis three series of EAI robot product lines on February 5, 2026.

It is reported that the company has set a goal of achieving positive operating cash flow and a gross profit margin of 20% within three years for this business.
