High-end models also feature a "Stardust Surround" grille, a dot matrix structure composed of 216 independent chrome accents, creating a galaxy-like lighting atmosphere, while the front bumper has also been optimized.

The side profile still maintains the classic large SUV silhouette of the GLS, with straight rooflines and waistlines ensuring a spacious interior. The roof rack and large multi-spoke wheels continue the model's luxurious presence.

The new car also adds a variety of paint color options, including Obsidian Black, Crystal Silver, and Emerald Green.

The changes to the rear are equally striking, featuring a through-type three-dimensional taillight sourced from the new E-Class, incorporating the three-pointed star element.

Inside, the new car is upgraded to the MBUX Superscreen ultra-wide screen, consisting of a 12.3-inch instrument cluster, a 14-inch central control screen, and a 14-inch passenger screen, paired with an in-car AI virtual assistant, seat vibration massage, a rear independent entertainment system, and a new steering wheel. The driver assistance system has also been updated.

In terms of power, the new car offers a 3.0T inline six-cylinder + 48V mild hybrid system, as well as a new 4.0T twin-turbo flat-crank V8 engine, and is standard with E-ACTIVE BODY CONTROL predictive air suspension.
