This block set aims to pay tribute to SEGA's iconic game console launched in the 90s. LEGO officials stated that the ingenious design allows players to use the same set of blocks to recreate either the Genesis or Mega Drive (European version) with different building methods, fully demonstrating the high flexibility and plasticity of LEGO blocks in creative design.

The set not only includes detachable controller components, allowing players to more realistically simulate the operating experience during assembly, but also carefully prepared retro-style decorative stickers to further enhance the visual effect of the model, making it closer to the classic look of the original console.

It is worth mentioning that before launching this "SEGA Genesis Console" set, LEGO had just released a new "Animal Crossing" themed block set.

The official set number is 40926, and the product description refers to it as a "highly detailed model." Whether players are loyal fans of the Genesis or prefer the unique styling of the Mega Drive, they can achieve personalized modifications through this non-functional model.

The addition of detachable controllers and decorative stickers highlights the craftsmanship in the details of the model, bringing a richer building experience for retro game enthusiasts.

Regarding release information, this SEGA Genesis console block set contains 479 parts and is priced at: £34.99 in the UK, €39.99 in Europe, and $39.99 in the US.
