Iran's chokehold on the strait and the U.S. blockade of Iranian ports have resulted in the loss of millions of barrels of oil to global markets and placed increasing pressure on the Iranian economy.

A senior U.S. official stated that U.S. forces, including warships and aircraft, actively protected several merchant vessels from Iranian attack on Monday. Crew members reported witnessing a drone successfully strike the oil tanker "JV Innovation," with military aircraft roaring overhead.

The U.S. military stated that it had successfully guided two U.S.-flagged commercial vessels through the Strait of Hormuz, marking the formal launch of Trump’s effort to restore international traffic through the strategic waterway. The senior official said the U.S. military did not provide close escort for these vessels, but instead used warships and aircraft to establish a defensive barrier to protect them.

The senior official said that a U.S. Navy missile destroyer supporting the defensive operation subsequently transited the strait. U.S. Central Command, responsible for the Middle East, stated that these warships are currently operating in the Arabian Gulf.

“U.S. forces are actively assisting in restoring the passage of commercial shipping,” Central Command said.

The last time U.S. warships entered the Strait of Hormuz was in early April, and it marked the first entry of U.S. naval vessels into the waterway since the outbreak of the war.
