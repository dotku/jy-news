It is reported that Apple will only launch the iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max, and the highly anticipated iPhone Ultra foldable screen in September, while the volume-selling iPhone 18 standard edition will be postponed until official release in the spring of next year.

This means that the iPhone 17 will become the longest-lived Apple phone in recent years. Since its debut in September last year, the phone has consistently been a blockbuster, and its dominance in the market will be further extended due to the postponement of subsequent models.

Compared to its predecessors, the iPhone 17's competitiveness has undergone a qualitative leap. The phone has for the first time adopted ProMotion high refresh rate technology, and its screen specifications are directly aligned with the Pro series, successfully making up for the core configuration shortcomings of the base model for many years.

In addition, Apple has also shown great sincerity in its storage solutions. The official version has canceled the 128GB entry-level version and directly started with 256GB, but the starting price remains at 5999 yuan. For most users, this entry-level configuration is already sufficient.

It is worth mentioning that the price of 5999 yuan is still within the national subsidy coverage. Currently, some regions support a national subsidy discount of up to 500 yuan, which means you can get this comprehensively upgraded quasi-flagship model for only 5499 yuan.

Thanks to this dimensionality reduction configuration and highly competitive price, the iPhone 17's market performance has been exceptionally bright. According to data from Counterpoint Research, its first month on the market drove a significant 22% increase in Apple phone sales against the trend.

At the same time, the overall mobile phone market declined by 2.7% during the same period. This stark contrast fully demonstrates the iPhone 17's strong appeal in the high-end market and has also accumulated sufficient market confidence for Apple's subsequent differentiated release strategy.
