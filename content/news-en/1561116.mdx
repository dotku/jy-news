According to tests by players and content creator Pixel Operative, the game's write speed during runtime can reach 15MB/s to 30MB/s, meaning it can write up to approximately 108GB of data to the SSD per hour. Continuous gameplay for 4 hours would result in an astonishing 432GB of data written.

Further testing shows that disk usage consistently surges to around 30MB/s when players move within their base or drive vehicles; read/write pressure only decreases when the character is stationary or in low-load areas.

Technical analysis suggests that this issue is not a simple bug, but may be related to the game's save system design. "Windbound Journey" uses a database structure based on RocksDB, and its relatively small write-ahead log (WAL) cache configuration may lead to frequent data refresh and compression operations, thereby amplifying the actual write volume.

In response to the controversy, developer Kraken Express has quickly released a fix patch. In the latest version 0.10.0.4, the official significantly reduced disk usage during gameplay. According to updated test data, the game's write speed has been reduced to 10MB/s to 16MB/s, and even below 1MB/s when the character is stationary, with an overall optimization of 60% to 75%.

Despite the issue being alleviated, this incident has once again sparked discussion among players regarding game optimization and hardware wear and tear.
