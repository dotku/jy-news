Although the function is currently not usable, the appearance of this placeholder icon is a strong signal that xAI is actively preparing to provide support for Apple's in-car system in the near future.

When users attempt to launch the app, a prompt message appears on the screen: "Grok voice mode is coming to CarPlay."

Notably, Grok is already a built-in feature of Tesla vehicles. This upcoming CarPlay support means the AI chatbot will break free from Tesla's limitations and enter almost all other brands of in-car systems, bringing a convenient interactive experience to a wider user base.
