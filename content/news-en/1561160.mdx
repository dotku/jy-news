As the most advanced strategic transport aircraft currently in China, the most prominent feature of the Y-20B in appearance is the four domestically produced large-diameter engines suspended under the wings. Due to their robust shape, they are vividly likened to carrying four large oil drums. This change in size is precisely a symbol of high-performance power.

Compared with the slender power units used in previous models, the diameter of the Y-20B engine has increased significantly. This is the most intuitive feature of the domestically produced WS-20 turbofan engine. A larger bypass ratio means better fuel efficiency, which can significantly increase the aircraft's maximum range.

At the same time, compared with the previously equipped power system, the thrust of the WS-20 has increased significantly. This makes the Y-20B's takeoff weight, payload capacity, and flight safety all comprehensively upgraded, and its operational reliability in complex environments has also become higher.

What appears to be a simple change in engine shape is actually a comprehensive and independent breakthrough in core technologies in China's aviation power field. By equipping a domestically produced heart, the Y-20B completely fills the last gap, allowing the Chinese Air Force to truly possess top-level long-range strategic delivery capabilities.

This leap-forward progress has also accumulated valuable experience for the self-reliance of power for China's civilian large aircraft. The mature application of the WS-20 will inevitably accelerate the process of equipping the domestically produced C919 passenger aircraft with Chinese engines. Currently, the Changjiang 1000A engine, which is used for civilian aircraft, is also undergoing intensive testing.

The takeoff of the Y-20B shows that China's aviation industry has overcome the barrier of the power heart. This means that future domestically produced large aircraft will no longer be subject to others and will be able to demonstrate the strength of Chinese manufacturing in the global skies with a more agile posture.
